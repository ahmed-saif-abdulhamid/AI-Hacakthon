# Payment Tracking Spreadsheet

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

This module gives you a single spreadsheet to log every invoice, its due date, follow-up actions, and payment status. You will be able to see at a glance which clients need a reminder and which invoices are already paid.

## Checklist

- [ ] Create a new spreadsheet using the provided template columns.
- [ ] Enter each invoice with client name, invoice number, amount, and due date.
- [ ] Set the initial status to "Sent" for every new invoice.
- [ ] Schedule the first polite reminder for three days before the due date.
- [ ] Send the reminder email using your standard template.
- [ ] Update the follow-up date column after each reminder sent.
- [ ] Change status to "Overdue" the day after the due date if unpaid.
- [ ] Send a second reminder at seven days overdue with a firmer tone.
- [ ] Log any client responses or promised payment dates in the notes column.
- [ ] Mark status as "Paid" and record the payment date when funds arrive.
- [ ] Archive rows older than 90 days to a separate sheet for reference.
- [ ] Review the spreadsheet weekly to prioritize follow-up actions.
