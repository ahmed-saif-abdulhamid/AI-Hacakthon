# Payment Timeline Tracker

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

The Payment Timeline Tracker helps freelance developers stay organized by clearly mapping key dates in the payment process. It ensures you don’t miss critical follow-up windows while maintaining professionalism and trust with your clients.

## Checklist

- [ ] Record the invoice date when you send the invoice to the client
- [ ] Note the original due date specified in the invoice or agreement
- [ ] Set a reminder for the first follow-up email, typically 3–5 days after the due date
- [ ] Send the first follow-up email within 3 to 5 days of the due date
- [ ] Log the date and content of the follow-up email in the tracker
- [ ] Identify if the client has responded or acknowledged the email
- [ ] If no response, schedule a second follow-up, typically 7–10 days after the first
- [ ] Send the second follow-up email with a clear, polite reminder of the overdue amount
- [ ] Log the date and content of the second follow-up in the tracker
- [ ] Check if the client has made a payment or responded to the second follow-up
- [ ] If still unpaid, prepare a formal payment request email with a clear deadline
- [ ] Send the formal payment request email within 7 days of the second follow-up
