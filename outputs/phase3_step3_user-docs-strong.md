# Late Payment Follow-Up Toolkit: how to use this toolkit

**Who it is for:** Freelance developers who send invoices and need a structured way to follow up on late payments without losing client relationships.

## What is inside

1. **Email Sequence Builder**: phase3_step2_module-1 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-1 (CSV)
2. **Payment Timeline Tracker**: phase3_step2_module-2 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-2 (CSV)
3. **Client Communication Style Guide**: phase3_step2_module-3 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-3 (CSV)

## How to use it

1. Read the module intro.
2. Copy the CSV template into your spreadsheet tool.
3. Work through the checklist in order and tick each step.

## What this toolkit does not do

- Automated email sending tools or integrations with specific platforms like Gmail or Zapier.
- Financial forecasting or revenue modeling features.
- Legal advice or contract drafting services.
