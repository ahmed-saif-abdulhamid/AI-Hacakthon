# Late-Payment Follow-Up Toolkit: how to use this toolkit

**Who it is for:** Freelance developers and designers who need a repeatable system to send invoice reminders and track overdue payments without awkward conversations.

## What is inside

1. **Reminder Sequence Planner**: phase3_step2_module-1 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-1 (CSV)
2. **Payment Tracker Dashboard**: phase3_step2_module-2 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-2 (CSV)
3. **Escalation & Resolution Guide**: phase3_step2_module-3 (checklist) and phase3_step2_template-3 (CSV)

## How to use it

1. Read the module intro.
2. Copy the CSV template into your spreadsheet tool.
3. Work through the checklist in order and tick each step.

## What this toolkit does not do

- Automated email sending or integration with accounting software
- Legal advice or custom contract clauses
- Client acquisition or pricing strategy content
