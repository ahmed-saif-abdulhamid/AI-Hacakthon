# Phase 2, step 1: Lean Canvas

Variant: Late-Payment Follow-Up Toolkit

Each statement cites verified claim IDs or is labelled ASSUMPTION by code.

## Problem

- Freelance developers and designers experience average payment delays of 23 days, creating cash flow gaps.[C6]
- Existing automated reminder tools and integrated invoicing platforms add recurring cost and complexity for solo freelancers.[C4]

## Customer Segments

- ASSUMPTION: Freelance web developers and designers who manage their own invoicing and follow-up.
- ASSUMPTION: Solo creative professionals who prefer low-cost, self-managed systems over SaaS subscriptions.

## Unique Value Proposition

- ASSUMPTION: A one-time purchase toolkit of templates and checklists for setting payment terms, scheduling reminders, and tracking overdue invoices — no monthly fees.
- ASSUMPTION: Designed by a freelance developer for freelancers, using familiar tools (Google Sheets, Notion) instead of a new platform.

## Solution

- ASSUMPTION: Downloadable toolkit with payment schedule templates, milestone trackers, deposit checklists, reminder email scripts, and a tracking spreadsheet.
- ASSUMPTION: Step-by-step guide for customizing terms, automating reminders with built-in spreadsheet features, and escalating overdue accounts.

## Channels

- ASSUMPTION: SEO-optimized landing page targeting searches for freelance invoice templates and late payment solutions.
- ASSUMPTION: Distribution through freelance communities, newsletters, and marketplaces like Gumroad.

## Revenue Streams

- ASSUMPTION: One-time digital product sale (toolkit) with optional paid updates.
- ASSUMPTION: Future upsell: advanced Notion workspace template or cohort-based workshop.

## Cost Structure

- ASSUMPTION: Domain, hosting, and landing page setup (~$50).
- ASSUMPTION: Payment processing fees (Stripe/Gumroad) and tax compliance (~5% per sale).

## Key Metrics

- ASSUMPTION: Waitlist sign-up conversion rate from landing page visitors.
- ASSUMPTION: Toolkit purchase conversion rate from waitlist subscribers.

## Unfair Advantage

- ASSUMPTION: Founder's 7 years of freelance web development experience and personal pain with late payments.
- ASSUMPTION: Expertise in Google Sheets and Notion enables building the toolkit entirely with no-code tools, keeping costs near zero.

## Riskiest assumption

- ASSUMPTION: Freelancers will pay for a static toolkit instead of using free templates or subscribing to automated SaaS reminders.

## Experiment

- ASSUMPTION: Launch a waitlist landing page describing the toolkit; measure sign-up rate and survey subscribers on willingness to pay $29–$49.

Pre-registered demand target: 100 waitlist sign-ups in 14 days (team default, ASSUMPTION).
