# Phase 2, step 1: Lean Canvas

Variant: Project Deposit & Milestone Tracker

Each statement cites verified claim IDs or is labelled ASSUMPTION by code.

## Problem

- ASSUMPTION: Freelancers lose time chasing late or missing milestone payments across multiple projects.
- ASSUMPTION: No simple, reusable system exists to standardize deposit collection and payment tracking for each engagement.

## Customer Segments

- ASSUMPTION: Freelance developers and designers who manage multiple client projects and need reliable payment workflows.

## Unique Value Proposition

- ASSUMPTION: A ready-to-use toolkit of templates and checklists that standardizes deposits and milestone tracking for every project.

## Solution

- ASSUMPTION: Downloadable set of templates, checklists, and guides for deposit agreements, milestone schedules, and payment tracking.

## Channels

- ASSUMPTION: Direct outreach to freelance communities, newsletters, and social channels where developers and designers discuss business operations.

## Revenue Streams

- ASSUMPTION: One-time purchase of the digital toolkit with optional updates.

## Cost Structure

- ASSUMPTION: Low production cost: founder time to create files, hosting for delivery, and minimal marketing spend within $300 budget.

## Key Metrics

- ASSUMPTION: Waitlist sign-ups and conversion rate to paid purchases.

## Unfair Advantage

- ASSUMPTION: Founder's 7 years of freelance web development experience and existing workflows built in Google Sheets and Notion.

## Riskiest assumption

- ASSUMPTION: Freelancers will pay for a downloadable file-based toolkit instead of building their own or using generic spreadsheets.

## Experiment

- ASSUMPTION: Launch a waitlist landing page describing the toolkit and measure sign-ups to validate demand before building the files.

Pre-registered demand target: 100 waitlist sign-ups in 14 days (team default, ASSUMPTION).
