Variant: Late Payment Follow-Up Toolkit

## Problem

- Freelance developers face repeated late payments and feel awkward sending follow-up reminders.[C74]

## Customer Segments

- ASSUMPTION: Freelance developers who send invoices and face late payments

## Unique Value Proposition

- ASSUMPTION: A no-automation, no-judgment toolkit to help freelancers follow up on late payments with clarity and ease.

## Solution

- ASSUMPTION: A downloadable toolkit with email templates and Notion checklists for follow-up.
- Includes a step-by-step guide for handling late payments without feeling awkward.[C74]

## Channels

- ASSUMPTION: Email
- ASSUMPTION: Notion templates

## Revenue Streams

- ASSUMPTION: One-time sale of a downloadable toolkit (set price at $29.99)

## Cost Structure

- ASSUMPTION: Development of templates and guides (estimated $200)
- ASSUMPTION: Design and formatting (estimated $100)

## Key Metrics

- ASSUMPTION: Number of freelancers who download the toolkit
- ASSUMPTION: Average time saved per follow-up interaction

## Unfair Advantage

- ASSUMPTION: Founder has 7 years of experience as a freelance developer and expertise in writing and Notion.

## Riskiest assumption

- ASSUMPTION: Freelance developers will find the toolkit useful and purchase it despite no software or automation.
