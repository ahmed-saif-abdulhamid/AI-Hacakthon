# Phase 1, step 4: Mom Test interview kit

For: aspiring entrepreneurs. Questions are about past behaviour only (no hypotheticals, no pitching).

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you had to find and share motivational quotes for social media posts. What happened?
2. What was the hardest part of that, and why?
3. What did you do about it? What tools, templates or people did you use?
4. What did that cost you, in money or time, the last time?
5. Have you looked for a better way? What did you find, and why did you not keep using it?
6. When did this last come up, and how often has it come up in the past three months?
7. Who else is involved when this happens?
8. Is there anything else about this I should have asked?

## Who to talk to (screener)

- They are one of: aspiring entrepreneurs.
- They had to find and share motivational quotes for social media posts at least once in the past three months.
- They are not a friend or family member (polite answers are not evidence).

## How to decide after five conversations

- **Strong signal:** 3 or more of 5 describe a recent, specific time it went wrong AND already spend money or real time on a workaround. Carry on to the waiting-list test.
- **Weak signal:** 1-2 of 5 do. Try another angle (a different segment or job).
- **No signal:** nobody can name a recent instance. Stop here; it saved you the build.
- Ask about the past only. Compliments and "I would buy that" don't count.
