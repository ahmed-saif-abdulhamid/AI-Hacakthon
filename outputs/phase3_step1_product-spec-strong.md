# Phase 3, step 1: Product spec

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

**Title:** Late Payment Follow-Up Toolkit

**Audience:** Freelance developers who send invoices and need a structured way to follow up on late payments without losing client relationships.

## Modules

1. **Email Sequence Builder**: Creates a step-by-step email sequence to follow up on late payments, tailored to different stages of delay and client types.
2. **Payment Timeline Tracker**: Helps developers track when invoices were sent, when payments were due, and when follow-ups should occur to avoid missing deadlines.
3. **Client Communication Style Guide**: Provides guidance on how to adjust tone and content based on client personality, past behavior, and relationship history.

## What the product is not

- Automated email sending tools or integrations with specific platforms like Gmail or Zapier.
- Financial forecasting or revenue modeling features.
- Legal advice or contract drafting services.
