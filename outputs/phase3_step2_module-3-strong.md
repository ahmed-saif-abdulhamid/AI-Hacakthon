# Client Communication Style Guide

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

Understanding your client’s communication style helps you tailor your messages to feel personal and respectful. Whether they’re direct or polite, busy or responsive, adapting your tone builds trust and increases the likelihood of a prompt payment.

## Checklist

- [ ] Review the client’s past communication patterns
- [ ] Identify their preferred communication channel
- [ ] Assess their history of payment behavior
- [ ] Determine their personality type—e.g., formal, casual, urgent, or collaborative
- [ ] Match your message tone to their established style
- [ ] Include a specific, personalized reference point from the project history
- [ ] Keep the message concise and action-oriented
- [ ] Avoid financial pressure or guilt-based language
- [ ] Add a brief, positive note about the project’s progress or value
- [ ] Offer a simple next step, such as a call or a quick reply
- [ ] Send the message within 24 hours of identifying the style
- [ ] Follow up with a brief, non-intrusive check-in after 48 hours if no response
