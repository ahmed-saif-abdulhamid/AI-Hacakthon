# Email & Message Templates

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

This module gives you ready-to-use copy for polite, firm, and final payment reminders across email and chat platforms. Each template is structured so you can drop in client details and send without rewriting from scratch.

## Checklist

- [ ] Choose the reminder stage that matches the invoice age (polite, firm, final).
- [ ] Open the corresponding template file for your preferred channel (email or chat).
- [ ] Replace placeholder fields with the client name, invoice number, amount, and due date.
- [ ] Add a clear call to action such as a payment link or reply instruction.
- [ ] Proofread the message for tone and accuracy before sending.
- [ ] Send the reminder from your business email or chat account.
- [ ] Log the send date, channel, and template version in your tracking sheet.
- [ ] Set a follow-up reminder for the next stage if payment is not received.
- [ ] Archive sent messages in a dedicated folder for future reference.
- [ ] Review response rates monthly and adjust wording if needed.
