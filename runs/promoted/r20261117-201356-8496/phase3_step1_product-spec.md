# Phase 3, step 1: Product spec

> PREVIEW after a founder override against the evidence at Gate 1 and Gate 2: demand not yet proven.

**Title:** Late-Payment Follow-Up Toolkit

**Audience:** Freelance developers and designers who need a repeatable system to send invoice reminders and track overdue payments without awkward conversations.

## Modules

1. **Reminder Sequence Builder**: Create a polite, escalating series of email and message templates that move clients from gentle nudge to firm deadline.
2. **Payment Tracker Dashboard**: Log every invoice, due date, reminder sent, and payment received in one spreadsheet so nothing slips through the cracks.
3. **Escalation Playbook**: Define clear steps for when reminders fail — late fees, contract clauses, and when to involve collections or legal help.

## What the product is not

- Automated email sending or CRM integration
- Legal advice or contract drafting service
- Client payment portal or invoicing software
