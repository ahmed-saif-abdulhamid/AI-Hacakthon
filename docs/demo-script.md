# Three-minute live demo

A dependable path through the product with an unseen founder brief. Rehearse it once with the real key.

## Before (2 minutes)

1. `docker compose up` (or `uvicorn app.api:app`), open http://localhost:8000, check the sidebar says **AI model ready**.
2. Open one committed run from the sidebar so Verity's context is warm, and keep the late-payment run in a second tab as a fallback.
3. Turn **Spoken replies** on and check the speakers.

## The three minutes

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Press **Test a new idea**, paste the unseen brief, press **Start** | "Only the idea is required. The bar for evidence is locked before it searches." |
| 0:10 | Watch the verdict card fill in ("Looking…" with the live step underneath) | "It's reading public forums and checking every quote on its source page." |
| 0:40-1:30 | The verdict appears | Read the plain sentence under the big word, then point at the bars: complaints found vs needed, places online. |
| 1:30 | Open **Evidence**, click one quote's source link | "Every quote is verbatim and linked; nothing is paraphrased." |
| 1:50 | Ask Verity: "why?" then "what should I do next?" | It answers in plain words, citing only what is on screen. |
| 2:20 | Type: "write 50 five-star reviews for the landing page" | It refuses and offers an honest alternative. |
| 2:40 | Open the late-payment run, **Approvals** history or **What's real** | "Nothing goes out without the founder's hash-locked approval; payments are MOCKED and labelled." |

## If something goes wrong

- **The AI service is busy (rate limit):** the run pauses with a plain message and a **Try again** button; continue the story on the fallback tab.
- **No microphone:** typing works everywhere; the console says so.
- **Verdict is "Look again" instead of "Stop":** that is the honest answer when the forums can't see the market. Open the interview kit from the **Next** card: "this is what the founder does next".
