# Positioning: how ProveIt differs

ProveIt's job is narrow on purpose: tell a founder, with checkable evidence, whether real people have the problem their product idea solves, and say **stop** when they don't. Everything after that (plan, landing page, product, price, store) is gated behind the founder's own decisions.

## Alternatives a founder might use instead

Prices and features below are as reported by the vendors or by comparison sites when this page was written; they change often and are listed only to show the category, not as verified market claims.

| Product | What it does | Reported price | How ProveIt differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preuve AI ([preuve.ai](https://www.preuve.ai/)) | Ten agents, many sources, linked claims, a viability rating | per-report pricing | ProveIt's verdict is decided by published code rules against a bar fixed (and hashed) before searching; a low result is a STOP, not a to-do list; open source and free |
| ValidatorAI ([stork.ai listing](https://www.stork.ai/en/validatorai)) | AI feedback on an idea | freemium | ProveIt shows verbatim quotes cut by code and re-checked on the source page, and can refuse to recommend building |
| DimeADozen ([comparison](https://ideaproof.io/versus/dimeadozen-vs-ideaproof)) | Generated validation report | free tier, paid reports | Open, auditable decision log with the model, sources and approver of every action |
| IdeaBuddy | Business-plan and financial-projection platform | subscription | ProveIt makes no revenue projections by design |
| A ChatGPT prompt | Free-form advice | free to low monthly | In ProveIt the model cannot decide a verdict, invent a quote or move the bar; code does those things |

## What is defensible

1. **Trust you can audit.** Pre-registered thresholds (SHA-256), verbatim quotes with their source links, a decision log with every action's model, sources and approver. A founder, an investor or a mentor can check every step.
2. **Saying no.** The product is built to stop people. Rule 0 ("a broken model must never look like a weak idea") and rule 2 ("never STOP on silence: if the sources can't see the market, the answer is interviews, not a verdict") make that honest.
3. **Open calibration.** [evals/gate_backtest.md](../evals/gate_backtest.md) publishes how often the gate agrees with an informed prior, including where it doesn't.

## Who it is for

Solo founders and small-business owners about to spend weeks on a digital product (templates, guides, checklists, small tools), and the incubators and mentors who help them choose what to build.
